Model & methodology

1. Unit of weight

The account weight is # CE's. Enterprise CE is de-duplicated at Country + Account. For individual AM capacity, two metrics are shown: CE Exposure (full CE of every account the AM touches) and Allocated CE (CE divided across active co-assignees). Allocated CE is a proxy only because the source does not contain a formal workload split.

2. Baseline

An active AM remains TPS-dependent until the latest Datanitiv implementation date among every account assigned to that AM. Recommended GWS date = latest blocker + selected stabilization buffer.

3. Fast-track

For every account with 2+ active AMs, exactly one person is designated temporary legacy holder. The optimization concentrates legacy responsibility on people already blocked later by other TPS accounts, minimizing total blocked-person-days. This can free other co-assignees earlier without changing the account's Datanitiv date.

4. Balanced

Same mechanism, but legacy-held CE per person is capped at . The cap is not a staffing standard: it is derived from the rounded P90 of current AM CE exposure to avoid excessive legacy concentration.

5. LOA handling

An AM on Leave of Absence is not allowed to be the legacy holder. Therefore a “shared” account may become effectively single-covered. That is why raw shared accounts and effective shared accounts are different.

6. Date acceleration

Acceleration is analyzed by critical bundle, not individual account. If an AM has two accounts on the same latest date, moving only one does not change their GWS readiness. The table therefore groups the accounts that must move together to produce an actual earlier release.

7. Important limits

This model does not know GWS licensing readiness, device policy, macro replacement status, Datanitiv UAT quality, account contractual restrictions, AM skill coverage, or workload complexity beyond CE. Those items should be treated as go/no-go gates before execution.
